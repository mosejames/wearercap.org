We are hands on

Volunteer hours

40 hours

Per family, per year

  • Log them in Track it Forward
  • EXP days, events, the merch room, concessions, field day, and more
  • Committee planning meetings count, including Zoom
  • Helping plan or run an event counts. Attending only as a parent does not.
Track it Forward

What was said

EXP is the Ron Clark Academy Experience: educators fly in from everywhere to watch our teachers teach, and they need us there. Passing out salads, pointing people to the bathroom, folding the thousands of shirts in the merch room. Two hours or eight, it all counts. Check in with Mr. Walker when you arrive so he knows what you came to do. Need an EXP shirt? Ask anyone there.

If you work 9 to 5: emails go out for evening and weekend jobs all year, some employers give paid volunteer time, and Holiday Decor alone can be 25 hours in one weekend. Anyone in your family can earn hours for you, as long as they are on your approved list with a volunteer button from the school.
