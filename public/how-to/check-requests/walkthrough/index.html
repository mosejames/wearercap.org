Illustrative TEST preview, not submitted

05 / sign in and submit

Certify, verify by phone, then submit.

The confirmation statements protect the budget and the payment record.

Practicing? You can review a sample and stop here. Submit only when the request and certifications are true.

06 / review queue

Review stays attached to the request.

A board reviewer signs in from the Board review tab.

They review the exact RCAP amount and documents, then approve or ask for changes.

An incorrect reviewer can be reassigned, with that action recorded in History.

Approval and payment are separate steps. The requester can follow both under Past requests.
