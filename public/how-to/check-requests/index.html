How to get reimbursed

Spent money on an RCAP committee or event? Send your receipts here and follow the request until you are paid.

Open check requests → Print this guide

wearercap.org/check-requests · About five minutes

Reimbursements, not advances. Buy within your committee's approved budget, keep the paid receipt, then submit. An order confirmation alone is not proof of payment.

Step by step

  1. Fill in the details

    • Go to wearercap.org/check-requests. You start on New request.
    • Enter your name and phone, then pick Reimbursement or Direct payment to vendor.
    • Choose your Committee and say what the expenses were for.
    • Payment is by Zelle. Enter the email or cellphone number your Zelle account uses.

  2. Add each expense and its receipt

    • For each purchase, enter the date, the Amount requested, and the receipt total.
    • Tap Attach paid receipts. PDF, JPG, or PNG, up to 5 files per expense, 10 MB each.
    • Choose all the receipts for one expense at once. Choosing again replaces them.
    • If only part of a receipt is RCAP, list the covered items and amounts.

  3. Sign in and submit

    • Enter your cellphone number and tap Text me a code, then type the code.
    • Check the two boxes confirming the expense is in budget and the receipts show payment.
    • Tap Submit request. You will get a request number and a text.

  4. Follow it to payment

    • Open Past requests anytime to see where it stands.
    • Awaiting approval, then Approved, then Paid. You get a text at each step.
    • If it says Changes requested, read the note, tap Update & resubmit, fix it, and send it back. It keeps its number.

Good to know

Stick with one sign-in

Sign in by text every time. Signing in with an email or Google creates a separate account, and your requests will not show there.

Want email instead of texts?

Add an email under Account & backup sign-in, then choose Email under Request notifications.

A PDF for your records

Check Email me a PDF copy when you submit, or tap Download PDF on any request.

Paying a vendor

Choose Direct payment to vendor and attach the unpaid invoice. Do not enter card numbers anywhere.
