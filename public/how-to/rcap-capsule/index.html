How to use the RCAP Capsule

One shared photo album for every all-school event. Add what you took, find what others caught of your kids, and say thank you.

Open the Capsule → Print this guide

wearercap.org/rcap-capsule · Two minutes to join

Everyone in the school can see, like, comment on, and download what you add. Share the moments you would want shared.

Step by step

  1. Sign in with your phone

    • Tap Sign in, enter your mobile number, and tap Text me a code.
    • Type the 6-digit code and tap Verify and continue.
    • Add your name and pick Your house. Your photos count toward your house on the leaderboard.
    • Tap Into the Capsule.

  2. Find an event

    • The Capsule opens on the latest event. Use Choose album to switch.
    • Tap any photo to see it full size. Swipe to move through, swipe down to close.
    • Tap the download icon to Save to Photos.

  3. Add your photos and videos

    • Tap Add photos and then Choose photos or videos.
    • Up to 60 at a time, 50 MB each. Photos and MP4 or MOV videos work.
    • Copies you already added are skipped automatically.
    • Keep the screen open until it finishes. Your phone's originals are not changed.

  4. Share and say thanks

    • Tap Invite to send the album link to your group chat so others add theirs.
    • Spot your kid in someone's photo? Tap Thank you, that's my kid! Only you and the photographer see it.
    • Your thank-yous and stats live in My Capsule. Tap your picture at the top.

Good to know

Albums open on the day

Each event takes uploads from 12:01 a.m. on event day and stays open. Before that the button says when it opens.

Remove your own photo

Open it, tap Delete, then Confirm deletion.

A photo of your child you want down

Open it, tap Report, and choose Please remove a photo of me or my child. RCAP reviews it.

House leaderboard

Tap Leaderboard in any album to see which house has shared the most.
