How to find a carpool

Only if it helps. Find RCA families who live near you and drive the same days, then plan the ride together.

Open carpool → Print this guide

wearercap.org/carpool · Completely optional

Your address is never shared. Other families see only your area, your names, and your schedule. Phone and email appear only inside a group you join.

Step by step

  1. Add your family

    • Tap Continue with Google first, the quickest way in. Or skip it and use your email.
    • Enter your name, your children's names, and A spot near you. Pick it from the list that appears.
    • Choose morning, afternoon, or both, and the days you need.
    • If you used email, type the 6-digit code we send and tap Verify my code.

  2. Wait for approval

    • RCAP approves each family before it can see others. Until then you see how many families are nearby.

  3. Find families near you

    • Open Explore nearby families to see who lives close and which days you share.
    • To look farther out, open Your account and move Show families within.

  4. Join a group and plan

    • Under Groups near you, check the box to share your contact info, then tap Request to join.
    • Once the organizer accepts, you see everyone's phone and email.
    • Tap Start our group text to get everyone in one thread and plan who drives when.

Good to know

Meet before the first ride

Get together first and ask the questions you would ask any parent you are trusting with your kid.

Use a public meeting point

A church, school, or store works well. Never put a home address as a meeting point.

Change your details

Go to Your account, then Edit my family. Tap Leave group to stop sharing with a group.

Want to start a group?

Email carpool@wearercap.org to be set up as an organizer.
