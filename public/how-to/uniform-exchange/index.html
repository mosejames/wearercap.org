How to use the Uniform Exchange

Uniforms that keep moving. Ask for a piece your student needs, or pass along what they have outgrown. No account needed.

Open the exchange → Print this guide

wearercap.org/uniform-exchange · Free for every RCA family

Save 404-566-7741 in your contacts. Every update comes by text from that number, and iPhones can hide texts from unknown senders.

Step by step

  1. Ask for an item

    • Tap I'm looking for an item.
    • Tap For a Girl or For a Boy, then pick your house, the item, and the size.
    • Tap Add to add it to your request. Up to 2 items per request.
    • Enter your name, your student and grade, and your cell number. Tap Send my request.

  2. Choose how it gets to you

    • Most pieces go Student to student: the bin holder's student brings it to yours at school.
    • Tap Send it with their student, or Decide later.
    • If nothing matches yet, you go on the waitlist and get a text when it comes in.

  3. Close it out

    • Once it is in your hands, open your text link and tap Got it.
    • Lost the link? Tap My requests, enter your number, and tap Text me my link.

Donating

  1. Offer what you have

    • Tap I have clothes to donate.
    • Enter your name, house, cell number, and what you have. Tap Offer it up.
    • Your house's bin holder texts you to arrange pickup.
    • Gently loved, freshly washed, stain-free, and still true in color.

Good to know

Who holds the bins

RCAP parent volunteers in each house. Your own house's bin is checked first.

Not sure of the size?

Ask anyway and say so in the notes.

Private by design

Your name and request are seen only by you, your bin holder, and RCAP.

Your link is a key

The link in your texts opens your requests. It does not expire, so keep it to yourself.
