A few things to know

Your listing.
Your way.

What should I have ready?

Your business or venture name, a short introduction, and at least one way to connect. Photos can be JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to 5 MB each. Social handles, a website, and a creation spotlight are welcome too.

Who can see my listing?

Published listings are public. Choose business contact details you are comfortable sharing. A parent manages student listings and their public contact information.

Can I change it later?

Absolutely. Sign in with the same account and open My listings to update photos, contact details, social profiles, or your featured creation. Save as a draft when you want to take it out of public view.

What if I have more than one venture?

You can create and manage multiple listings with the same account. Give each business or venture its own introduction.
