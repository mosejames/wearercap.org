A note on participation

Participating doesn't mean always agreeing.

It means engaging thoughtfully and constructively in the work.

This is permission. We want candor. Different perspectives help us think more broadly. Pause here and let it land.

The formal role

A real voice in real decisions.

When the Executive Board asks, you can

  • Recommend and support policies and activities
  • Initiate policy proposals
  • Share your opinion on matters brought to you

When governance calls, you may help with

  • Nominations and elections
  • Audit oversight
  • Policy discussions and bylaw review
  • Programs that enhance RCA's instruction

These responsibilities are established throughout the RCAP bylaws.

This is where parents get a voice in decisions that affect the RCAP and RCA community. Not every item comes up every year. When one does, we will ask.

Our commitment to you

It works both ways.

Keep you informed

Updates, meeting materials and a clear view of what's happening across RCAP.

Seek your perspective

We'll ask for your insight where your experience can shape our thinking.

Make space for candor

Honest, constructive feedback and different views are welcome here.

Close the loop

We'll follow up on your input and share where things landed.

This is our side of the deal. If we ask for your input, you will hear back about what happened with it. Invite them to hold us to it.

Six everyday moments. The pattern: information goes out, questions and ideas come back, and when in doubt, ask. Next two slides let the group try it.
