WE ARE RCAP

wearercap.org Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

At a glance

We use information to run community tools, verify access and preserve shared memories.

Some galleries are publicly viewable. Signing in to upload does not make photos, comments or reactions private.

Videos are uploaded separately through Dropbox. Contact us to request help or removal of your child’s information.

1. Who we are

We Are RCAP provides community information and tools for Ron Clark Academy families, chaperones, volunteers and organizers at wearercap.org. This policy covers sign-in, family coordination tools, forms, trip vaults, galleries and related communications. External services have their own privacy policies. The website is operated by RCAPers, the Ron Clark Academy parents, a parent-volunteer group serving as the community’s PTSA. This parent-led website is not owned, operated, sponsored or endorsed by Ron Clark Academy. For privacy questions, contact privacy@wearercap.org.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the feature, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, family or student details, carpool or volunteer information, comments, reactions, photos, videos or other submissions. Organizer tools may hold event records, receipts and administrative submissions.

We process account identifiers, verification and session information, upload and download activity, and interactions with features. Providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information and request logs to deliver and protect the service.

Media may identify children, family members or staff. Files can contain embedded metadata, including capture dates and GPS location. Photo processing creates viewing copies and may retain a capture date. Some original or retained files may preserve metadata, including location; do not assume all uploads or downloads have location information removed. Review location settings and share only media you are authorized to provide.

3. Google sign-in and Google user data

The trip-vault Google sign-in requests basic identity, email and profile permissions: openid, email and profile. It receives your Google account identifier, name, email address and available profile information, such as a profile photo URL. This information is handled through Supabase Auth and associated website account records to authenticate you, maintain your session, identify contributions and link accounts when you request it. Google sign-in does not give us your Google password, Gmail messages, Drive files or Calendar events.

We share Google sign-in information with service providers only as needed to operate and secure these features, and may disclose it when legally required or necessary to protect users or the service. Contributor identity may be displayed with contributions as explained below. We do not sell Google user data, provide it to data brokers, or use it for advertising or ad targeting.

Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access through your Google Account settings. Revocation does not automatically delete information already retained by the website. Contact us to request deletion.

4. Other access and browser storage

Some features use verification codes sent to a phone number or linked email. Linking Google to an existing phone account can keep earlier contributions associated with the same account.

Access requirements vary. Some content can be viewed without an account, and some features remember contributor information or permissions in your browser. Browser storage also maintains sessions, preferences and reaction identity. Clearing it may sign you out or affect your ability to manage earlier contributions. External services may use their own cookies or storage.

5. How we use information

We use information to provide requested tools; verify identity and access; associate contributions with their owners; display community information and shared media; coordinate family and volunteer activities; send requested codes and service communications; support comments, reactions and appreciation; respond to requests; moderate content; prevent abuse; and maintain reliability.

Some gallery tools record views and interactions using a browser identifier. Newsletter tools can record tracked-link activity to help organizers understand engagement. These records support service operation and engagement reporting.

6. Visibility, comments and reactions

Access differs by feature. Some pages and galleries, including the current Washington, DC trip gallery, can be viewed without signing in. People with gallery access may see contributor names, photos, comments and reaction counts, share links, or download available media. Links may be forwarded and downloaded copies may remain outside our control.

Notes from home is a chaperone appreciation view that brings together comments and reactions on that contributor’s photos. Those comments remain visible on the corresponding gallery photos; this is not a private-message inbox.

Administrative tools use the access configured for those features. Review the collection screen before submitting information and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

7. Service providers and separate video uploads

Core services include Vercel for website hosting, Supabase for authentication and application records, Cloudflare R2 for gallery media, and Google for Google sign-in. Communications and other features may use additional providers identified in the relevant feature. Providers process information to deliver their services under applicable arrangements and their own policies.

Trip video buttons open a separate Dropbox file request. Videos and the name, email or other information requested by Dropbox go to the request owner’s Dropbox collection. They do not automatically appear in the photo gallery. Dropbox’s privacy policy also applies.

We do not share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in information with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process that information to deliver requested messages and operate the service.

We may disclose information to meet legal obligations, address abuse, or protect users and the service.

8. Retention and removal

Trip and community photos and videos are preserved beyond the event until removal is requested. We retain account and submission information while needed to provide the associated services and handle requests. Technical logs and verification records are retained only as needed for operation, security and troubleshooting, subject to provider retention settings. Archiving a trip does not itself delete its media or records.

Where available, you can remove your own photos or comments from the visible gallery. Removing a visible entry does not necessarily delete stored files or backups. We assess deletion requests across relevant records, media and providers, subject to applicable legal and security needs.

Parents and guardians may request removal of a child’s photo or information even when someone else uploaded it. Provide the gallery link or enough detail to locate the content and explain your relationship to the child. We may need to verify the request. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive documents.

Contact the privacy contact listed below for access, correction, account deletion, media removal or other privacy requests. We cannot recall copies already downloaded or shared by others. Rights may depend on applicable law.

9. Children and student information

Family and trip tools are intended for parents, guardians, chaperones and authorized organizers. Students do not submit directly. Parents, guardians, chaperones and authorized organizers submit content and are responsible for having the necessary permission to share student information, photos, videos and anecdotes.

If you believe a child has provided information without appropriate authorization, or have a concern about a child’s media, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

10. Security and changes

We use authentication, access controls and other technical measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee complete security. Providers may process information in locations different from where you live.

We will display the effective date and update this policy when practices change. Where required, we will provide notice and obtain consent before new uses of information.

11. Contact

RCAPers (Ron Clark Academy parents), a parent-volunteer group. Privacy questions and removal requests: privacy@wearercap.org.
