What it looks like in practice.

When

the Executive Board sends something parents need to know

Share it with your class.

When

several parents are asking you the same question

Bring it back to us.

When

you hear an idea that could make an RCAP activity better

Share it.

When

parents have questions or concerns

Help their voices reach the right place.

When

we're considering something that affects families

Help us see what we might be missing.

When

something isn't clear to you

Ask. Other parents likely wonder too.
