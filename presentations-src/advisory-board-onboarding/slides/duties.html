Your responsibilities

What we're asking of you.

01

Be the liaison

Share what the Executive Board sends. Bring back what your class says.

02

Show up and speak up

Come to meetings ready to engage. Your ideas, experiences and questions count.

03

Advise and give feedback

Be a sounding board. Ask questions and help us see what we might miss.

04

Grow parent engagement

Help families feel informed, connected and invited to take part.

05

Support RCAP governance

Step in when the Advisory Board is called on for its formal duties.

Walk the five quickly; the next slides go deeper on the formal role. On number one: you are the two-way connection. On number two: participation is active, not a seat on a call. On number four: RCAP's goals include encouraging parents to be involved in their children's education and supporting classroom instruction and school programs.
