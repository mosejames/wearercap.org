Buy $1 raffle tickets

Share a little. Make a difference.

The raffle runs October 19 through November 16. We invite each family to sell at least $100 in tickets. Share the link or QR code with family, friends and coworkers, and ask them to include your child’s name, class and house so your student receives credit.

Top ticket sellers will be recognized by house and class. Thank you to every family and donor supporting Teacher Appreciation Week.

Your Sunday drawing guide.

One prize will be drawn each Sunday listed below. The entry deadline for each weekly drawing is the preceding Saturday at 11:59 p.m.

Sunday, October 25Enter by Saturday, October 24
Sunday, November 1Enter by Saturday, October 31
Sunday, November 8Enter by Saturday, November 7

A weekly win keeps you eligible for future drawings, including the grand prize.

Follow RCAP on Instagram for the winner announcement video. The team plans to post it by 8:30 p.m. each drawing Sunday. Winners also receive an email.

The student with the most contributions each week will help announce that week’s winner in the video.

Ticket sales end November 16. The grand-prize drawing date is still to be announced while the team waits for the basketball schedule.

Prizes and prize donations

Announced prizes include gift cards, a gaming system, Hawks tickets, Falcons tickets, a television and more.

Have an item or a connection to share? Contact Brittany Mosely about a prize donation and donation documentation. Local businesses and parent-owned businesses are welcome to contribute.

Thank you to our generous prize donors for helping us celebrate the RCA faculty and staff.
