A CHANGE OF PLANS · NOVEMBER 16 UPDATE

But the celebration isn’t over.

Thanks to your incredible support, we raised approximately $25,000 for the school community who give so much to our children every day. Thank you for every ticket purchased and every share.

Due to technical difficulties with our raffle platform, the November 16 General Body Meeting was canceled and the Fall Raffle drawing was rescheduled. Our raffle team reported that Zeffy’s data exports continued to be affected by the previous week’s Microsoft outage. Without the export needed to prepare the drawing, we were unable to hold it as planned.

Your raffle data is safe. The delay concerns access to the export needed to prepare the drawing. Ticket sales are closed.

We’re taking the excitement to the basketball court. Join us on December 4 for our LIVE Fall Raffle Drawing during halftime of both the girls’ and boys’ games. We’ll announce our lucky winners and celebrate our amazing Raffle Committee, top fundraising families, house, and class.

It is our first home game of the season, against Chattahoochee Hills. The girls tip off at 5:30 PM and the boys at 6:45 PM. Any livestream details will be shared once confirmed. Final fundraising totals and rankings will be posted after reconciliation. Earlier published updates remain below.

Also, please save the date: January 25, 2027, for our next virtual RCAP General Body Meeting.
