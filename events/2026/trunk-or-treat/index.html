TRUNK OR TREAT · OCTOBER 30

Ready, set, treat.

An afternoon of costumes, candy and decorated trunks for all RCA 4th through 8th graders.

WHENFriday, October 302:30 to 3:30 p.m.
WHERERCA CourtyardAll 4th through 8th graders

A little candy goes a long way.

Families are welcome to donate bags of individually wrapped, nut-free candy. Donations are appreciated, not required.

Friday, October 23Collection begins at 3:30 p.m.
Monday, October 263:30, 4:30 and 5:30 p.m. carpool
Tuesday, October 273:30, 4:30 and 5:30 p.m. carpool

Drop donations with parent volunteers during student pickup. Extra candy will be shared among the trunks and activities.

MAKE IT EXTRA SWEET

Find your way to help.

Host a trunk, 1 to 4 p.m. Three trunk hosts are requested. Bring your Halloween decorating ideas and help with an activity beside your trunk.

Help with an activity, 2 to 4 p.m. Parents can also support candy collection, serving treats, supplies and cleanup. You’re welcome to choose more than one volunteer slot.

Decoration reimbursement: Trunk hosts can receive up to $50 back. Keep your actual receipt to be reimbursed. Volunteer hours are available.

Look forward to decorated trunks, ring toss, a candy corn guessing jar and more fun fall activities.
