# RCAP Parent Orientation Welcome Card: Design Handoff

*Prepared Sept 7, 2026 for a designer. Orientation is Thursday, Sept 10. This needs to print by Wednesday.*

---

## What we're making

A printed piece handed to every parent at Ron Clark Academy's Parent Orientation on Sept 10. Roughly 400 families. It goes to returning parents and new parents alike, so it cannot read as a "welcome to the school" card. It is RCAP's introduction of itself to the whole parent body at once.

Physical format: a bifold on heavy cardstock, four panels (front, inside left, inside right, back). If a bifold is too much for the content, a single two-sided card is acceptable, but the content below assumes four panels. Small enough to keep, not a flyer.

---

## Who RCAP is

RCAP is Ron Clark Academy Parents, the parent organization at RCA in Atlanta. Every parent with a child at RCA is already a member. There are no membership tiers and nothing to join. RCAP funds and runs teacher appreciation, the fall raffle, holiday decorating, trunk or treat, the uniform exchange, a dads' group, parent socials, and community service, and supports the school with volunteers at major events.

The 2026-27 board is new and is rebuilding the organization around one idea: RCAP should be the opposite of a clique. Accessible to every family. Every seat open.

---

## The intent of the piece

Two jobs, in this order.

**1. Make membership feel real.** Most parents don't know what RCAP is, or think it belongs to the people who are always in the building. The card's first job is to say, in a person's voice, you are already RCAP. This is a message, not a directory.

**2. Be a resource parents keep.** After the message, it answers the questions parents actually ask in September: who represents me, what happens this year, when are the meetings, how do I get involved. Nothing on it should be true only for a week. Anything that changes lives on wearercap.org, and the card points there.

What it is not: a fundraising ask, a dues reminder, a sign-up sheet, or a school document. No dollar figures anywhere. No "join us" language.

---

## Voice and writing rules

- Plain, warm, direct. Written by a person, not an organization.
- Short sentences. Two short ones beat one long one.
- **No em dashes anywhere.** Use periods, commas, or colons.
- No exclamation points in body copy.
- Never call the reader a "new parent." Every parent gets this card.

---

## Brand

- Ink: `#1a1613`
- Paper: `#faf4ea`
- Gold: `#f7a81c`
- Orange: `#f26a1b`

Editorial feel, not corporate. The website (wearercap.org) uses warm paper backgrounds, strong typographic hierarchy, and diagonal band transitions between sections. Match that spirit. Gold and orange are accents, not fills.

RCAP does not have a finished logo yet. Use "RCAP" set in type, or "We are RCAP" as the mark. Do not invent a logo.

The school uses a four-house system (Amistad, Isibindi, Altruismo, Rêveur). Don't use house colors or names on this card. RCAP sits above the houses.
