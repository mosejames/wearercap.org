Georgia GOAL helps fund scholarships for students. For RCA families, it’s a way to support financial aid at the school we love through a Georgia state income tax credit.

THE IDEA

Support students. Receive a state tax credit.

Eligible Georgia taxpayers can contribute to GOAL and receive a dollar-for-dollar Georgia income tax credit for an approved contribution, subject to program rules and limits. You can designate RCA to support scholarships for eligible students.

A tax credit reduces tax owed; it is different from a tax deduction. Your available credit depends on your situation and approval. Read GOAL’s official FAQs ↗

HOW IT WORKS

Start with the official application.

  1. Learn & apply

    Visit GOAL’s donor page for the current application and details. Choose the Ron Clark Academy as your school designation.

  2. Wait for approval

    GOAL coordinates the state preapproval process. Follow its approval notice for your approved amount and payment deadline.

  3. Contribute & keep your receipt

    Make your approved contribution as directed by GOAL, then use its tax receipt when claiming your credit.

GOAL’s current application is for the 2027 tax-credit cycle, with signup during 2026. December 18 is the RCA signup deadline shared by Ms. Wooten. Follow GOAL’s approval notice for the contribution deadline.
