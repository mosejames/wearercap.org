01. Organizer and purpose

The 2026 Fall Raffle is organized by RCAP. Ticket proceeds support RCAP’s efforts for its community. Teacher Appreciation Week is a principal focus of this fundraiser; proceeds are not represented as being restricted exclusively to that activity.

02. Ticket purchases and entry records

Tickets are offered at $1 each through RCAP’s linked Zeffy checkout. The announced sales period is October 19 through November 16, 2026. A photo invitation or shared link is an invitation to participate, not a raffle ticket or a confirmation of entry.

Complete the purchase through checkout and retain the confirmation and ticket information provided there. Review the information, eligibility confirmations and transaction terms shown at checkout before purchasing. Questions about a purchase or ticket record should be directed to the raffle chairs listed below.

03. Student, class and house credit

If a family invited you, enter the student’s name, class and house in the corresponding checkout fields so the raffle team can attribute the purchase. The invitation page displays those details and offers a copy button.

Creating, sharing or opening an invitation does not automatically enter those details into Zeffy or record a ticket sale. Please check the attribution information when completing your purchase. Student credit and prize entry are separate: student details identify the family supporting the fundraiser; the purchaser’s checkout record identifies the ticket purchase.

04. Announced weekly drawings

One prize is planned for each announced weekly drawing. Entry for a weekly drawing closes at 11:59 p.m. Eastern on the preceding Saturday.

05. Prizes and winner notifications

Announced prizes include gift cards, a gaming system, Hawks tickets, Falcons tickets, a television and additional prizes. The announced list does not assign a particular prize to a particular drawing or establish a prize’s value.

RCAP plans to announce weekly winners on its Instagram account and notify winners by email. The team’s planned posting time is by 8:30 p.m. on each drawing Sunday. Participants should keep the email address used at checkout available for communications from the raffle team.

06. Personalized photo invitations

Families may create a photo invitation with the student’s name, class, house and family name. Anyone with the shared invitation link can view the invitation. Invitations are not listed in a public photo gallery.

Only use photos you have permission to share. The generated invitation card is used as the shared link’s preview image. The creator can hide the invitation from the browser used to create it. Hiding a link cannot recall images or messages that recipients have already saved or forwarded.
